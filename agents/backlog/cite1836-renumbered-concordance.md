# A concordance for the 24 sheets left on the renumbered streets

Why: 24 Vasserot sheets on the rue d'Arcole (11), the rue de Constantine (9) and the rue de la Cité (4) still find no parcel: the plans' index gives them no second address, so their footprints are not drawn.

Done when: a source giving the 1836 address or parcel of each house is found and written in DATA_SOURCES.md, and the sheets it covers are matched (fit and scale still checked); or the item is blocked with what was searched.

Notes:
- These sheets are dated 1842 to 1851, after the streets were cut or widened (rue d'Arcole 1837, rue de Constantine in the 1840s). Some draw houses rebuilt on new lots, which will not fit an 1836 parcel and are not the Cité of 1836. Check the date of the building before using a sheet for the 1836 view; a later date needs its own start year in the model.
- The index's corrected numbers ("en fait") were tried in PR #23 and do not help: they are a later numbering.
- Clues already in the index's `detail` notes, not used yet: corners ("angle de la rue Cocatrix", "angle de la rue des Marmousets et de la rue Constantine"), side streets without a number ("et rue de la Licorne"), owners' names (the same owner sometimes has a sheet under the old street name: "Juiverie (rue de la) n°27 … en fait : 37 rue de la Cité" gives the pair old 27 = new 37 directly). Collect those "en fait : N rue de la Cité / rue Constantine / rue d'Arcole" notes on the old streets' sheets first: they are a concordance in the reverse direction.
- Other places to look: the sheets' margins, the Bottin of 1836–1840 (SoDUCo directories), Lazare's Dictionnaire des rues (1844).
- Shape alone does not work (PR #17). A corner note narrows the candidates to the parcels that touch both streets; with the scale check that may be enough for some.
