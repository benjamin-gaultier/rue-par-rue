## Albert-Kahn licences

Checked on 2026-11-21 for the 1900–1929 wall textures (backlog item `era1920-kahn-licence`).

**What the sources say.** The dataset's own metadata gives "Licence Ouverte (Etalab)" for the index and sends images to the museum's [terms of use](https://collections.albert-kahn.hauts-de-seine.fr/cgu_media) (the link in the metadata, `/cms/cgu_media`, is dead). The terms put reusable images under CC0 or CC BY 4.0. Both allow copying, changing and publishing, also for sale. The terms add the author's moral rights: give the title and the author's name, and do nothing to the work that distorts it or changes its meaning. The museum also asks, without requiring it, for the caption, the inventory number, its full name with "Département des Hauts-de-Seine", a link to its site, and to be told of reuses.

**Which licence a photo has.** The open data does not say CC0 or CC BY. Its only rights field, "Libre de droits" (`droits_cessibles`), is "Non" on 482 Paris records (still under copyright, says the field's description) and empty on the other 3,820. The museum's collections portal shows the licence of each image, and there it follows the operator. Still images on the portal on 2026-11-21: Gadmer 20,722, Chevalier 10,219 and Dumas 8,707, all CC BY 4.0; Léon 12,602 CC0 and 10 CC BY; Cuville 3,584 and 50; Passet 4,288 and 5. `rpr kahn` writes one of three values, so the "CC0 or CC BY 4.0" value is reasoned, not read from the record:

| Value in `photos.geojson` | When | Paris photos | Dated 1900–1929 | Textures and generated facades | Credit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `CC-BY-4.0` | Operator is Gadmer, Chevalier or Dumas | 1,197 | 1,176 | Allowed | Operator, title, inventory number, "Musée départemental Albert-Kahn, Département des Hauts-de-Seine", "CC BY 4.0", and what was changed ("cut and straightened", "generated from") |
| `CC0-1.0 or CC-BY-4.0` | Operator is Léon, Cuville or Passet | 2,623 | 2,542 | Allowed, under the stricter of the two: treat as CC BY 4.0 | The same |
| `Not cleared for reuse (open data: libre de droits = Non)` | "Non" in the open data: no operator named (414) or Lachalarde (68) | 482 | 276 | Not allowed. The app links to the image on the museum's portal and copies nothing | — |

A spot check of 19 photos against the portal: the 12 cleared ones show the licence written here; the 7 marked "Non" were not found on the portal under their inventory number.

**What this means for the walls.** A texture cut from a cleared photo is a changed copy: allowed, with the credit above on the wall. A facade generated from cleared photos is a new image made from them: allowed by both licences, as long as it is labelled as generated, credits the photos it came from, and is never shown as the operator's own picture (the moral-rights clause, and this project's standing rules). Photos marked not cleared are used for neither.

**Image size.** The open data serves images up to 800 px on the long side, with no account. The portal's large format (2,560 px) needs an account and accepting the terms: the owner's step, not a pipeline's.

## Checked but not used yet

- **Gallica (BnF)** IIIF: rich in Atget prints, postcards and plans. Commercial reuse of images is paid.
- **Albert-Kahn films** (21 Paris films in the same dataset).
- **SoDUCo trade directories** (1787–1914, geocoded): planned for shop names on the 1836 facades.
- **APUR building construction periods**: planned for hiding buildings that did not exist yet.
- **IGN 1980–1995 mosaic**: no tiles over central Paris.
- **État-major 1:10,000 "environs de Paris"**: leaves the city itself blank.
