Paris · 1790 → today Rue par Rue

Île de la Cité in 3D The whole island and the facing quays are drawn in detail: facades, roofs, gardens and trees, the quays, and each bridge as it stood that year, generated from each building’s age, height and roof type. Further out, plain blocks in the colours below. Demolished houses come from Vasserot’s cadastre, a few traced from his plans; their heights and facades are estimates. Click a photo or a building. Drag the year through the 1860s to watch Haussmann clear the island, one building site at a time.
